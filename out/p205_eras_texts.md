One car. Three eras. 🦁 The Peugeot 205 – and the 208 that followed it.

🟥 1983 – Peugeot 205: its rally twin, the 205 Turbo 16, won the World Rally Championship manufacturers' title in 1985 and 1986 – and the Paris–Dakar in 1987 and 1988. 5.28 million 205s were built.
🟦 2012 – Peugeot 208: in 2013 Sébastien Loeb's 875 bhp 208 T16 climbed Pikes Peak in 8:13.878 – about a minute and a half faster than the old record (9:46.164).
🟨 2024 – Peugeot 208 II (facelift): this generation ended the VW Golf's long reign as Europe's best-selling car in 2022 (JATO Dynamics). Also available fully electric as the e-208.
