One car. Three eras. – Chevrolet Corvette
• 1953 – the first Corvette: fiberglass body, 150 hp six-cylinder, only 300 built – every one of them in Polo White.
• 1997 – C5 Corvette: new 5.7-litre LS1 V8 with 345 hp, and the gearbox moved to the rear axle (rear transaxle).
• 2020 – C8 Corvette Stingray: the first mid-engine production Corvette, 495 hp and 0–60 mph in 2.9 s (with Z51 package / performance exhaust).
Colours shown: Polo White (1953 – the only colour that year), red (C5, original photo colour – Torch Red was a 1997 factory colour), Rapid Blue (C8, a 2020 factory colour, original photo colour). Photos show a 2002 C5 and a 2020s C8 of the same generations.
